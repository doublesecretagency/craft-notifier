## Volume Filters

Volume filters limit the notification to a chosen set of asset volumes. The asset's stored volume must appear in the selection for the notification to dispatch.

<img class="dropshadow" src="/images/events/volume-filters.png" alt="" style="width:350px; margin-top:10px">

:::warning Selection is required
At least one volume must be checked. If no volumes are selected, nothing will be sent out.
:::
